# NAOqi fidelity

**Status:** Implemented

## Purpose

naoqi-sim behaves like a real NAOqi robot in its API, but its NAOqi is the desktop build from the user's Choregraphe suite, not a robot's. This spec is where the two differ: which modules each image runs compared with a real NAOqi robot of the same version (today a NAO: V4/V5 on 2.1, V6 on 2.8), every difference the desktop build shows, and what naoqi-sim does about each one (keeps it, provides what is missing, could provide it, or lives with it). The images and their boot are in [container.md](container.md); the services naoqi-sim replaces or adds have their own specs ([service-replacement.md](service-replacement.md) for the mechanism).

## Decided

### Matching a real NAOqi robot's modules

Each image matches a real NAOqi robot **on the same NAOqi version**, not the other version's image: real robots differ between 2.1 and 2.8 too. The reference is the robot's own `autoload.ini` in the public robot image (`/etc/naoqi/autoload.ini` on 2.1, `/opt/aldebaran/etc/naoqi/autoload.ini` on 2.8), compared with the desktop suite's (measured Oct 9, 2026):

| Difference between the two images | Cause | On real robots too? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.8 only: `ALMood`, `ALBackgroundMovement`, `ALListeningMovement`, `ALSpeakingMovement`, `ALAnimationPlayer`, `Conversation`, `Knowledge`, `ALUserInfo`, ... | 2.8's `dialog`, `expressivity` and `life` packages | Yes |
| 2.1 only: `ALBonjour`, `ALRedBallTracker`, `ALSegmentation3D`, `ALCloseObjectDetection` | Dropped in 2.8 | `ALBonjour` and `ALRedBallTracker`, yes. `ALSegmentation3D` and `ALCloseObjectDetection` (and `ALWavingDetection`), no: they need a depth camera, and a NAO 2.1 serves none of the three (measured on a real NAO, Oct 16, 2026; see the depth camera below). `ALWavingDetection` goes with the replaced people perception on 2.1 ([perception-stubs.md](../services/perception-stubs.md)) |
| `ALBasicAwareness`, `ALAutonomousMoves` and the hidden `_ALExpressiveness`, `_ALAutonomousBlinking` missing on 2.1 | The 2.1 desktop suite ships these libraries but leaves them out of its `autoload.ini` | No: naoqi-sim adds them to its autoload copy ([container.md](container.md), "Entrypoint") |

- Entries the robot autoloads that the desktop suite ships but does not load: 2.1 `expressiveness`, `basicawareness`, `autonomousblinking`, `autonomousmoves`, `aldiagnosis`, `facetracker`, `visionrecognition`, `alchestbutton`, `memorywatcher`, `notificationreader`, `voiceemotionanalysis`, `mecalogger`, `dcm_hal`; 2.8 `aldiagnosis`, `memorywatcher`, `voiceemotionanalysis`. Each loads with `launchLocal` on a running image, which stays healthy (`voiceemotionanalysis` and `mecalogger` register nothing).
- The 2.1 entrypoint adds the four that make the autonomous abilities the robot runs on 2.1 (expressiveness, awareness, blinking, autonomous moves) to its autoload copy, in the robot's order, with `autonomouslife` after them, since the robot loads it after them and it may depend on them ([container.md](container.md), "Entrypoint"). The others are added when a client needs them (open questions); vision modules are not wanted.
- Entries the robot autoloads that the desktop suites do not ship at all (audio input, `ALSystem`, face detection, speech recognition, sound localization, photo and video capture, landmark and barcode detection, infrared, laser...) cannot be added. naoqi-sim provides `ALAudioDevice` and `ALFaceDetection` itself ([face-detection.md](../services/face-detection.md)) and replaces the built-in `ALPeoplePerception`, which detects nothing on the desktop, with its own ([people-perception.md](../services/people-perception.md)); the modules that only ran on the built-in's modularity graph become API-only stubs ([perception-stubs.md](../services/perception-stubs.md)); `ALSystem` stays absent (its absence tells a client the target is not a real robot); speech recognition and the other detectors are the clients' ([_overview.md](../_overview.md), "Perception").

### Measured against a real NAO 2.1 (Oct 16, 2026)

A read-only inventory (the service directory, each service's `metaObject`, ALMemory's key and event lists, `getSubscribers`) of a NAO V5 on NAOqi 2.1.4.13, compared with the same inventory of the 2.1 image, headless: 117 services on the robot, 87 in the sim, 77 shared. **The 77 shared services have the same public methods on both**, except naoqi-sim's own replacements: our `ALAudioDevice` lacks 15 of the robot's methods (audio-device.md, open question 3) and exposes 11 `ALModule` internals a robot's does not (`BIND_PYTHON`, `autoBind`, `getModule`, `registerToBroker`...), and our `ALTextToSpeech` lacks `getLanguageEncoding` and `sayToFileAndPlay`. The robot's `ALTextToSpeech` declares six ALMemory events ours does not (`ALTextToSpeech/TextStarted`, `TextDone`, `TextInterrupted`, `CurrentSentence`, `CurrentWord`, `PositionOfCurrentWord`) and a `synchroTTS` signal (speech.md); the robot's `ALAudioDevice` has a `speakersPlaying` signal. The 30 public services only the robot has are the hardware and perception ones this spec already names, plus `ALAutomaticVolume`, `ALMecaLogger`, `ALMemoryWatcher`, `ALRobotHealthMonitor`, `AudioFilterLoader`, `Daps` and `DialogUtilities` (the last two from installed packages); `ALVoiceEmotionAnalysis` is absent on the robot too. The robot has 7479 ALMemory keys to the sim's 3149: the 2.1 virtual robot has the `Device/SubDeviceList` joint boards but only 7 keys per joint against the robot's 79, and no `Face`, `Ears`, `ChestBoard` (LEDs) or `InertialSensor` detail; its 354 events against the sim's 220 are the absent services' and the installed packages'.

The experiments that followed, on the robot at rest and awake, each reversed, and the same calls on the 2.1 image where a comparison was wanted:

- **`wakeUp()` stands a NAO up**: from Crouch at rest, `wakeUp()` left the robot in `Stand` (stiffness on, posture `Stand`, family `Standing`), and `rest()` returns it to Crouch; the 2.1 image does the same.
- **Awareness** (`ALBasicAwareness.startAwareness()` with life disabled, the head only): it subscribes to `PeoplePerception/PopulationUpdated`, `MovementDetection/MovementDetected`, `ALSoundLocalization/SoundsLocated` and `TouchChanged`, and to the modules `ALPeoplePerception` and `ALMovementDetection` at 200 ms and `ALSoundLocalization` at 100 ms (`getSubscribersInfo`), which take the top camera at QQVGA and QVGA; the 2.1 image's awareness does the same without the sound (naoqi-sim's 2.1 has no `ALSoundLocalization`). `stopAwareness()` releases them all.
- **Silencing by `pause(True)`** on the robot's `ALPeoplePerception`, `ALMovementDetection` and `ALSoundLocalization`: `isPaused` true, `isProcessing` false, the subscription kept, no event in 5 s; `pause(False)` resumes. A client that does its own perception silences the robot's this way ([_overview.md](../_overview.md), "Perception and speech recognition").
- **Feeding awareness from a client works on the robot as on the sim**: a fake person published as the people-perception contract (keys plus `PopulationUpdated`) is tracked within a second (`StimulusDetected "People"`, `HumanTracked 42`, the head turning to its yaw, `HumanLost` 2.2 s after it leaves); `_triggerStimulus([x, y, z])` turns the head to the point (`StimulusDetected "CustomStim"`, on the robot and the 2.1 image alike); `FrontTactilTouched 1.0` plus `TouchChanged [["Head/Touch/Front", true]]` raised from a client gives `StimulusDetected "Touch"`. A movement event was not fed: no real `MovementDetection/MovementDetected` was captured to learn its payload.
- **Speech durations**: the robot's `say()` and the 2.1 image's, `TextStarted` to `TextDone`, for the same five sentences: 0.81 and 0.79 s, 4.23 and 4.18 s, 8.17 and 7.88 s, 4.52 and 3.90 s (French), 1.09 and 1.12 s (the engine's `tts` within 14 % of a NAO's Nuance voices; [speech.md](../services/speech.md), open question 3).
- **Postures and walking, with room around the robot**: `goToPosture` at speed 0.6 from Stand reaches `StandInit`, `StandZero` and back in 1.1 to 1.5 s, `Crouch` in 2.6 s, `Sit` in 14.9 s and Stand again from Sit in 13.6 s, each answering true with the family `Standing`, `Sitting` (and `Standing` in Crouch, above); a `PostureFamilyChanged` event accompanies each change. `moveTo(0.2, 0, 0)` walks 0.201 m forward in 5.2 s on the odometry, drifting 4 cm sideways and 0.13 rad; `moveToward(0.6, 0, 0)` covers 0.07 m in its first 2 s (the gait ramps up: 1.5 cm at 1.2 s, 6 cm at 2.1 s), so the 3 to 5 cm the sims cover in 2 to 3 s are of the robot's order, and a speed test needs a longer walk. `stopMove()` then `waitUntilMoveIsFinished()` returns within 20 ms, `moveIsActive()` false.
- **Animated speech, standing** (English, `ALAnimatedSpeech.say`): a sentence with an explicit `^start(animations/Stand/Gestures/Hey_1)` and `^wait` takes 6.37 s (`TextStarted` 0.38 s after the call, the animation's preparation; speech 5.94 s; `EndOfAnimatedSpeech` 44 ms after `TextDone 1`); the same plain sentence takes 5.11 s in `contextual` mode, 4.43 s in `disabled` and 4.57 s in `random` (speech itself 4.99, 4.40 and 4.44 s: contextual mode slows the speech by about 13 %); the robot ends in `StandInit`. `EndOfAnimatedSpeech`'s value counts the calls.
- **Movement events**: `ALMovementDetection` subscribed at 200 ms raises `MovementDetection/MovementDetected` about four times a second while a person waves (44 in 12 s), but its value reaches a qi subscriber as nothing, on the host (libqi 3) and inside the robot's own Python alike, and the key reads empty: the payload's format could not be learnt from the robot, so feeding awareness a movement stays untested.
- **Autonomous life on** (`setState("solitary")`, with room around the robot): the robot stands up and awareness runs with the same four inputs, and the launchpad plugins join in: `_LaunchpadPluginPerceptionHeavy` subscribes to `ALPeoplePerception` (directly and through `ALGazeAnalysis`), `ALMovementDetection`, `ALGazeAnalysis` and `ALEngagementZones` at 200 ms and to `PeoplePerception/PopulationUpdated`, `GazeAnalysis/PersonStartsLookingAtRobot` and `ALBasicAwareness/HumanTracked`; `_UserSessionManager` to `PopulationUpdated` and `HumanTracked`; `fall-recovery/plugin` runs. `setState("interactive")` is refused ("Invalid state transition: solitary to interactive"): the robot enters `interactive` when an interactive activity takes the focus (`switchFocus("run_dialog_dev/.")` did, for 3 s, the dialog's `ALSpeechRecognition` cycling `Status ListenOn`/`Stop`/`ListenOff`, `ActiveListening`, `SpeechDetected 0` and a `WordRecognized ["", -3.0]`, then back to `solitary`). Back to `disabled`, the robot keeps its posture (`rest()` is the client's), awareness and the plugins' subscriptions are gone, but the two helper behaviors the dialog focus started (`dialog_say_something_else/bhr_set_pushmode`, `dialog_shutdown/bhr_shutdown`) stay running until stopped. `ALSoundLocalization/SoundLocated` carries `[[seconds, microseconds], [azimuth, elevation, confidence, energy], [head position, 6 floats], ...]` (one real sound seen).
- The audio, video, player and touch measurements are in their specs' open questions: [audio-input.md](../host/audio-input.md) 1 and 3, [audio-device.md](../services/audio-device.md) 1 to 3, [audio-player.md](../services/audio-player.md) 3, [touch-input.md](../host/touch-input.md) 1, [video-input.md](../host/video-input.md) ("Purpose").

### Desktop NAOqi facts

How the desktop NAOqi differs from a real robot's, and what each difference means for naoqi-sim:

- **By design**: naoqi-sim keeps the difference on purpose.
- **Patched**: naoqi-sim provides what the desktop lacks.
- **Patchable**: naoqi-sim could provide it, with the lever named. It is a candidate for a plan once a client needs it.
- **Limitation**: the behavior lives inside a large module naoqi-sim cannot replace, and no input it could provide is known to change it. It stays a limitation of naoqi-sim, with its workaround. Patching or decompiling Aldebaran's binaries is never a lever.

The levers, cheapest first: load or configure what the suite ships; publish the data in ALMemory; replace the service with ours from the autoload ([service-replacement.md](service-replacement.md)), worth it for an API naoqi-sim can implement fully; stub it; provide the input a built-in lacks (a stand-in DCM).

- **By design.** No `ALSystem` service and no version key in ALMemory; `RobotConfig/Body/Type` is absent (2.1 and 2.8). The version (and the fact that the target is naoqi-sim) must come from a naoqi-sim service.
- **Patched.** No `ALAudioDevice` on either version (naoqi-sim adds one, [audio-device.md](../services/audio-device.md)); `ALAudioPlayer` is a stub that spawns `/opt/naoqi/bin/sndfile-play`, which fails in Docker (naoqi-sim replaces it, [audio-player.md](../services/audio-player.md)). No face engine, so no `ALFaceDetection` and an `ALPeoplePerception` that publishes nothing (naoqi-sim adds the one and replaces the other on its `perception` container, decided Oct 29, 2026: [face-detection.md](../services/face-detection.md), [people-perception.md](../services/people-perception.md)); the vision modules that read the built-in's population (`ALEngagementZones`, `ALGazeAnalysis`, `ALSittingPeopleDetection`, 2.8's `ALWavingDetection` and `_UserIdentification`) are API-only stubs ([perception-stubs.md](../services/perception-stubs.md)): their API answers, they detect nothing, as before.
- **Patchable (publish in ALMemory).** 2.1 has `Device/SubDeviceList/*` keys in ALMemory; the 2.8 virtual robot has none, having no DCM. So on 2.8 the joints' temperature keys (`Device/SubDeviceList/<joint>/Temperature/Sensor/Value`, which the Motor Heat and Get Temperature boxes read) are missing (measured Oct 13, 2026). naoqi-sim could publish plausible temperatures, and joint positions from `ALMotion.getAngles`; a stand-in DCM would provide them too (open question 2).
- **Patchable (lever to find). No LED state.** Neither virtual robot has the LEDs' actuator keys (`Device/SubDeviceList/Face/Led/Red/Left/0Deg/Actuator/Value`, ...): `ALLeds` accepts every command (`fadeRGB`, `fade`, `on`, `off`, `setIntensity`, `randomEyes`...) but `getIntensity` raises "Data not found", so a client cannot read the LEDs back (2.1 and 2.8, measured Oct 13, 2026). On a real NAO 2.1 the actuator keys follow every `ALLeds` command within the fade (`fadeRGB("FaceLeds", green)` reads red 0, green 1, blue 0; `off("ChestLeds")` 0, 0, 0; `setIntensity("EarLeds", 0.25)` reads 0.125, the ears' scale; `reset` restores the defaults), because `ALLeds` writes to the DCM and the DCM mirrors its actuators into ALMemory (measured Oct 16, 2026); the chest LED is also driven by another module (it turned yellow by itself while off). The 2.1 virtual robot's simulated DCM has no LED devices (no `Face`, `Ears` or `ChestBoard` keys at all, where the robot has 480, 200 and 50), so on both versions the lever is the same: naoqi-sim publishes the actuator values `ALLeds` commands imply, or replaces `ALLeds` (31 methods), which would also let the sim window show the LEDs (open question 2).
- **Patchable (publish in ALMemory). No body id.** `Device/DeviceList/ChestBoard/BodyId` (the Get Body ID box) is missing on both versions; naoqi-sim could publish a fixed one.
- **Not a difference: a test fault. Collision protections follow their setters**, on both versions as on a real NAO 2.1, one `ALMotion` cycle later. What was recorded as a limitation on Oct 14, 2026 was the tests reading the getters right after the setters and with Pepper's names. Measured Oct 16, 2026, on the 2.1 image and on the robot, at rest and awake: `setCollisionProtectionEnabled("Arms", False)` makes `getCollisionProtectionEnabled("LArm")` and `("RArm")` false about 30 ms later (the next motion cycle), and `("Arms", True)` restores them; `setExternalCollisionProtectionEnabled("Move", True)` makes `getExternalCollisionProtectionEnabled("Move")` true 30 ms later, and `"All"` sets `"Move"`; `"Arms"`, `"LArm"` and `"RArm"` read false whatever is set, on the robot too: a NAO has one external protection, `Move` (the arms' is a Pepper's), and the Set External Anti-Collision box's default body part does nothing on a NAO. The live tests poll the getter for up to a second (`settles`).
- **Not a difference: a real NAO does it too. 2.1: `ALRobotPosture.getPostureFamily()` says "Standing" in Crouch** (after `goToPosture("Crouch")` or `ALMotion.rest()`, also 1.5 s later), while `getPosture()` says "Crouch"; 2.8 says "Crouching" (measured Oct 14, 2026). A real NAO on 2.1.4.13 answers the same, resting in Crouch: `getPosture()` "Crouch", `getPostureFamily()` "Standing" (measured Oct 16, 2026). So this is NAOqi 2.1's behavior, which the live tests expect (`tests-e2e/test_motion_live.py`); a client wanting the posture reads `getPosture()`.
- **The other way: more than a NAO. 2.1: the virtual robot has a depth camera.** `ALVideoDevice.hasDepthCamera()` is true, `getCameraIndexes()` gives `[0, 1, 2]` and `getSubscribers()` lists `FrameGetter/Depth_0` and `Depth_1` besides the two head cameras, and the suite loads the depth modules `ALSegmentation3D`, `ALCloseObjectDetection` and `ALWavingDetection`; a real NAO 2.1 has `[0, 1]`, no depth camera and none of the three modules (measured Oct 16, 2026). A client that lists the cameras or the services sees a Pepper-like extra. Workaround: a client written for a NAO ignores camera index 2. Whether the virtual robot's depth camera can be left out is not investigated.
- **Limitation. 2.1: `ALAutonomousLife.setState` is refused** ("Calls to the setState method are not currently allowed", measured Oct 29, 2026, on the desktop suite with or without naoqi-sim's modules), so Autonomous Life is exercised on 2.8 only, where `solitary` stands the robot, runs awareness and opens user sessions on tracked people. Not investigated further.
- **Patched. 2.8's awareness tracking needs the fast person tracking process.** `ALBasicAwareness` starts tracking a person by setting the frequency of `FastPersonTracking_process` in `ALModularity`; with the tracking library stubbed the call failed and awareness ignored every later person (measured Oct 29, 2026). The library is kept, with the shared sources it reads loaded by naoqi-sim ([perception-stubs.md](../services/perception-stubs.md), "The tracking processes").
- **Limitation. The launchpad's perception plugin is not shipped.** `_LaunchpadPluginPerceptionHeavy`, which writes `Launchpad/ZoneOfTrackedHuman` and `PeopleInZoneOne` for the activities' launch conditions on a robot, is in neither desktop suite (2.8 ships the Activities, Battery, HardwareInfo, Posture, Temperature and Timing plugins; 2.1 only `_AutonomousLaunchpad`), so zone-based activity launching does not exist on the sims whatever the perception does.
- **Limitation, for now. 2.8: `ALRobotPosture` cannot reliably reach "Sit".** The 2.8 desktop suite has no DCM, so its virtual robot has no inertial sensor (2.1's simulated DCM reports the torso upright), and the posture manager reads the lean of the Stand-to-Sit transition as a perturbation (`PostureManager: Perturbation during gotoPosture` in the log). Measured Oct 13, 2026, `ALMotion.wakeUp()` and `setMaxTryNumber(3)`, `goToPosture("Sit", 0.8)`: from Stand, `False` after 16 to 25 s, usually still in `Stand`, sometimes in `Sit` or `Unknown`; from Crouch, `Sit` reached each time but `True` only some of the time (15 to 21 s). Stand and Crouch work, as every move does on 2.1. Workaround: check `getPosture()` rather than the result, go through Crouch, or test Sit on 2.1. What would change it is a stand-in DCM providing an inertial sensor (open question 2).

## Open questions

1. **More added built-ins.** The other shipped-but-not-autoloaded modules (2.1: `aldiagnosis`, `memorywatcher`, `alchestbutton`, `notificationreader`, `facetracker`, `visionrecognition`; 2.8: `aldiagnosis`, `memorywatcher`) are left out until a client needs one.
2. **A stand-in DCM, and where `ALLeds` writes.** Several 2.8 differences may share a root, the missing DCM: the `Device/` keys and joint temperatures, Sit, and possibly the LED state and the collision protections. Whether a minimal stand-in DCM (a module named `DCM`, present before `ALMotion` starts) would lift them, at what cost, and where `ALLeds`'s commands go on each version (a `qicli trace` while it fades), are to investigate before deciding each one ("Desktop NAOqi facts").
