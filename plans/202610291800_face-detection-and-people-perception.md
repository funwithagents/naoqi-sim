# Face detection and people perception: the perception engine, `ALFaceDetection`, the `ALPeoplePerception` replacement and the stubs

**Status:** Done

Implements the settled behavior in [perception-engine.md](../specs/container/perception-engine.md), [face-detection.md](../specs/services/face-detection.md), [people-perception.md](../specs/services/people-perception.md) and [perception-stubs.md](../specs/services/perception-stubs.md), and what they changed in [config.md](../specs/runtime/config.md) ("The perception engine"), [api.md](../specs/runtime/api.md) (steps 3 and 4, "Images", `status()`), [container.md](../specs/container/container.md) ("Compose", "Entrypoint"), [service-replacement.md](../specs/container/service-replacement.md) (the left-out libraries, "Overloads, structs and properties"), [status-service.md](../specs/container/status-service.md) (the two perception keys), [naoqi-fidelity.md](../specs/container/naoqi-fidelity.md), [viewer.md](../specs/host/viewer.md) (the naoqi-viewer pin) and [ci.md](../specs/testing/ci.md) (the archive). It delivers a `perception` container running OpenCV's YuNet, `ALFaceDetection` added inside NAOqi on both versions, `ALPeoplePerception` replaced with a population built from the faces, API-only stubs for the modules the built-in library took with it (with a typed `_UserIdentification` on 2.8), the `perception.enabled` config, the images built, verified and checked as the others, live tests driven by face props, and the specs flipped to `Implemented`. It leaves out face recognition (empty stubs), real engagement zones, movement detection and the `Launchpad` keys (which the desktop suites cannot have). The survey it builds on, with its prototype stubs, is `spike/perception_survey/` (`spike/RESULTS.md`, "Face detection and people perception survey").

3. **The face module** (`face-detection/`): the `Detector`, the engine client, `FaceDetected`; then **the people module** (`people-perception/`) on its detections. Check by hand with the live tier's config and a face prop: `FaceDetected` angles against the pinhole, a person at the prop's distance, the viewer's marker on the portrait, awareness turning the head.
4. **The host**: the config block, the compose environment, the build, verification and check of the third image, the waits, `status()`.
5. **The fast tier** (below), then **the live tier** on both versions; `AGENTS.md`'s map; `ci.yml`.
6. **Measure and record** in the specs' "As measured" paragraphs: the detection period held at 5 and 10 Hz on both versions under emulation, the engine's time per frame, the distance error of a prop at 1 and 2 m, the image's size.
7. The statuses: the four specs `Implemented`, the eight back to `Implemented`, this plan `Done`.

## As built

Done Oct 29, 2026, on both versions: the fast tier (425 tests) and the live tier (203 passed, 11 skipped, 21 expected failures before the three fixes below; the three affected files then 23 passed and 1 expected failure, life on 2.1, which refuses `setState` on the desktop) pass. What the build found, and changed from the plan:

- **2.8's tracking processes are kept, not stubbed.** `ALBasicAwareness` sets the frequency of `FastPersonTracking_process` in `ALModularity` itself; with the library stubbed it tracked once and then ignored every person. The library read only the shared segmentation settings the people library loads, so a C++ autoload entry (`naoqi_sim_perception_sources.cpp`) loads `peopleperception_sources.mds` right after `modularityperception`, and `fastpersontracking` and `findpersonhead` load as the suite ships them ([perception-stubs.md](../specs/services/perception-stubs.md), "The tracking processes").
- **`_UserIdentification.store` answers `0`**, a status: answering the user id made the session manager store the person again and never focus a user.
- **People are matched by world position** (0.35 m), not by angles: a head turned by awareness changed every face's angles and gave the person a second id.
- **The detector makes its `ALVideoDevice` and `ALMotion` proxies at first use**: both load after our modules, and a proxy made at load failed the face module's import on 2.1.
- **The defaults are the built-in's**, read from `peopleperception_sources.mds`: 2 s before a person is invisible, 10 s before they leave, 3.5 m of range.
- **Stubs never process** (`isProcessing` false whoever subscribes).
- naoqi-viewer's life-size face plane merged as `8d696b8` (its PR 6), the pin.
- Two live tests that encoded the Oct 9 decision were updated (`test_absent_live`, `test_status_live`).
